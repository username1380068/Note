Uniform Domination by Iterated Pullbacks

Let XX be a normal projective variety over an algebraically closed field, and let f:X→Xf:X\to X be a finite surjective endomorphism. Fix an ample Cartier divisor HH and a rational number λ>1\lambda>1 such that f*H−λHf^*H-\lambda H is ample. Such a choice is available when ff is int-amplified. All divisor inequalities below are coefficientwise.

Proposition 1. The following statements hold.

  1. For every ℚ\mathbb Q-Cartier ℚ\mathbb Q-divisor DD, there exists an integer m>0m>0, independent of ii, such that mλ−i(fi)*H−Dis ample for every i≥0.m\lambda^{-i}(f^i)^*H-D \quad\text{is ample for every }i\ge0.

  2. For every Weil ℝ\mathbb R-divisor DD, there exists an integer m>0m>0 such that, for every i≥0i\ge0, there is an effective ℚ\mathbb Q-Cartier ℚ\mathbb Q-divisor EiE_i satisfying D≤Ei∼ℚmλ−i(fi)*H.\boxed{\quad D\le E_i\sim_{\mathbb Q}m\lambda^{-i}(f^i)^*H.\quad}

Proof. Set A=f*H−λHA=f^*H-\lambda H. For every i≥1i\ge1, telescoping gives (fi)*H−λiH=∑j=0i−1λi−1−j(fj)*A.(f^i)^*H-\lambda^iH =\sum_{j=0}^{i-1}\lambda^{i-1-j}(f^j)^*A. Every term on the right is ample, since ff is finite. To prove (i), choose an integer m>0m>0 such that mH−DmH-D is ample. Then mλ−i(fi)*H−D=(mH−D)+mλ−i((fi)*H−λiH)m\lambda^{-i}(f^i)^*H-D =(mH-D)+m\lambda^{-i}\bigl((f^i)^*H-\lambda^iH\bigr) is ample for i≥1i\ge1, and the assertion for i=0i=0 holds by construction.

For (ii), choose an effective Cartier divisor C≥DC\ge D. Indeed, if D+D_+ is the positive part of DD, then for a≫0a\gg0 a nonzero section of 𝒪X(aH−⌈D+⌉)\mathcal O_X(aH-\lceil D_+\rceil) gives such a divisor C∈|aH|C\in|aH|. Apply (i) to CC and choose the resulting integer mm. For each i≥0i\ge0, the ample ℚ\mathbb Q-Cartier divisor mλ−i(fi)*H−Cm\lambda^{-i}(f^i)^*H-C has an effective ℚ\mathbb Q-linearly equivalent representative GiG_i: take an effective member of a sufficiently divisible very ample multiple and divide by that multiple. The divisor Ei=C+GiE_i=C+G_i is effective and ℚ\mathbb Q-Cartier, and satisfies the required inequality and ℚ\mathbb Q-linear equivalence. ◻

Remark. The conclusion concerns a representative of a ℚ\mathbb Q-linear equivalence class; it does not assert the literal inequality mλ−i(fi)*H≥Dm\lambda^{-i}(f^i)^*H\ge D for the fixed representative HH. For example, on ℙ1\mathbb P^1 take f(z)=zqf(z)=z^q, H=[∞]H=[\infty], D=[0]D=[0], and 1<λ<q1<\lambda<q. Then mλ−i(fi)*H=m(q/λ)i[∞]m\lambda^{-i}(f^i)^*H=m(q/\lambda)^i[\infty] does not dominate [0][0] for any mm, although its class admits a representative that does. If DD is not ℚ\mathbb Q-Cartier, statement (ii) still applies, while mH−DmH-D need not be ℚ\mathbb Q-Cartier.
