Valuative Decay of Normalized Pushforwards

PDF · TeX

Let XX be a normal projective variety over an arbitrary field kk. For a Weil ℝ\mathbb R-divisor BB and a prime divisor FF on a normal projective birational model p:Y→Xp:Y\to X, write vF(B)=coeff⁡F(p*B)v_F(B)=\mathop{\mathrm{coeff}}_F(p^*B), where p*p^* denotes the asymptotic pullback. We use its monotonicity and its agreement with the usual pullback on ℚ\mathbb Q-Cartier divisors. All divisor inequalities are coefficientwise; intersections of Cartier divisors with Weil divisors are understood as intersection numbers of cycles.

Proposition 1. Let f:X→Xf:X\to X be a finite surjective int-amplified endomorphism. For every effective Weil ℝ\mathbb R-divisor DD and every divisorial valuation vFv_F over XX, one has limi→∞vF((fi)*Ddeg⁡(fi))=0.\lim_{i\to\infty} v_F\!\left(\frac{(f^i)_*D}{\deg(f^i)}\right)=0.
